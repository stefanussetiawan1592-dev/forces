1. Forces in Everyday Life

Forces are everywhere around us! Here is a quick reminder of how forces act in the real world:

Tension Force

Pulling force applied through ropes or cables.

Gravitational Force

Pulling objects straight downwards toward Earth.

Frictional Force

Opposing the relative motion between surfaces.
