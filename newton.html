Newton's First Law (Inertia)

An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a non-zero net external force.

$$\sum \vec{F} = 0 \implies \vec{v} = \text{constant}$$

Newton's Second Law (Acceleration)

Acceleration is directly proportional to net force and inversely proportional to total mass.

$$\sum \vec{F} = m \cdot \vec{a}$$

Newton's Third Law (Action-Reaction)

For every action force, there is an equal and opposite reaction force acting on interacting bodies.

$$\vec{F}_{\text{action}} = -\vec{F}_{\text{reaction}}$$
